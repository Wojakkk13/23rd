Bilog,

Twenty-three months. Somehow that number still surprises me, because most days it just feels like "us", like something that was always going to happen, sooner or later, whether we planned it or not.

I don't have some big grand thing to give you today, just this simple thing I made instead of buying you a gift for now that you'd have to find room for. It's small, like most of what I give you, but it's built out of every version of you I've fallen for since September 22nd — the nervous one on our first date, the one who fights fair, the one who still laughs at my worst jokes like they're new every time.

Thank you for staying easy to love, even on the days I probably wasn't. Here's to the 24th, and the one after that, and however many it takes.
